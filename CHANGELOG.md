# Changelog

All notable changes to the taxonomy, following [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the release rules of [`taxonomy.md` § Extension](taxonomy.md#extension).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- Rules of the taxonomy: areas, domains, subdomains, stable IDs, reserved slots, placement, facets, extension.
- Registry 0.1.0: 10 areas, 36 domains and 74 subdomains.
- Facet vocabularies 0.1.0: kind, role, phase, stack, scope, status, source, quality.
- `scripts/check_registry.py` with its unit tests, and the local CI (just, mise, lefthook).
